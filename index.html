FAQ

Questions parents ask first.

What does Trainer cost parents?

Nothing to get matched. You pay your coach directly at their normal rate. We earn a small placement fee from the trainer when you book — it doesn't change what you pay.

How fast will you respond?

Within 24 hours, usually faster. We're a small team — Logan answers most messages himself, so you're not getting bounced through a chatbot.

What ages and sports?

We're starting with football, ages 8–18, anywhere in Utah. Other sports coming soon. Tell us what you need and we'll get the right coach.

Do you help with college recruiting?

Yes. Several of our coaches specialize in recruit prep — film breakdowns, position-coach intros, and helping showcase your athlete to college programs. Tell us your timeline and we'll match accordingly.

Can you do group or team sessions?

Most of our coaches offer both 1:1 and small-group rates. Group sessions are typically $30–$50 per kid. We can also coordinate team training if you've got a youth or high school squad.

How do you vet trainers?

Background check, three reference calls with prior families, verification of any coaching certifications or playing history, and a video intro. We turn down most applicants.

What if my kid doesn't click with the coach?

Tell us within the first two sessions and we'll re-match you at no extra cost.

Can my kid's existing coach join?

Yes. Have them visit this page and join the network. We'll get them in front of more families.
